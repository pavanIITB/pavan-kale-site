---
title: How to ask for feedback on a rough idea
description: Specific questions that help you improve an unfinished sketch.
date: 2026-11-26
sample: true
---

Start with the piece of the idea you are willing to change. If you are comparing two possible layouts, show both in the same rough form. Ask where the person would start and what they expect to happen next. Give them space to explain without filling in the answer.

When you hear a vague reaction, ask them to point to the part that prompted it. “This feels complicated” might refer to a crowded page, an unfamiliar word, or the order of the steps. You can work with any of those once you know which one they saw.

Write down their reasoning in their own words and decide what to test. One person’s preference can be an outlier. Several people reaching for the same control or misunderstanding the same label is worth investigating, especially while moving it still takes a pencil stroke.
