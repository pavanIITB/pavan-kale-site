---
title: Keeping the prototype simple
description: How to decide what the first prototype needs to prove.
date: 2026-11-26
sample: true
---

Before building a prototype, write down the question it should answer. A bottle that lights up can tell you whether a reminder is noticeable. A finished enclosure can wait until you’ve learned where the light should go and whether it’s easy to see.

Make the roughest version you can test safely and repeatably. Keep a record of what changed between versions. When something improves, that record helps you understand the change well enough to keep it.
