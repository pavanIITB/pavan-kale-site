---
title: Shipping the first Sipwise bottle
description: How a problem I kept running into became the first Sipwise smart bottle.
date: 2026-11-27
---

I used to sit down to work with a full bottle next to me and go hours without taking a sip. The water was already there. What I needed was a reminder I’d actually notice.

At IIT Bombay, I started working on a bottle that would give me that reminder itself. It took months of prototyping to get to the point where the bottle could light up and prompt me to drink. From there, we kept adjusting it until we had something we could ship.

I remember holding the first finished Sipwise bottle. Getting a prototype to work had taken persistence. Getting it into someone else’s hands had taken a team. I felt proud of what we’d made and relieved that it was finally out in the world.
