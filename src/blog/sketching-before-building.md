---
title: Sketching before building
description: Using a quick drawing to make an idea easier to discuss.
date: 2026-11-26
sample: true
---

Draw a rough version of what you have in mind and show it to someone before you explain it. Ask what they think each part does. The mismatch between your intention and their reading gives you somewhere to start.

Try a second sketch with one meaningful change. Put the two versions next to each other and talk about what each makes easier. A drawing doesn’t need to look polished to make a design decision clearer.

Keep the sketch next to the decision it helped you make. A week later, a team may remember that it chose a layout but forget what problem the layout was supposed to solve. A photo of the sketches, with one sentence about the test or conversation, can preserve enough context to revisit the choice when the design changes.
