---
title: Starting with a personal problem
description: A way to turn an everyday frustration into a useful product question.
date: 2026-11-26
sample: true
---

Start by writing down the last three times the problem happened. Where were you? What were you doing? What did you try, and where did that approach fall short?

The answers might change what you build. Forgetting to drink during a long stretch of work, for example, is a different problem from being unable to find water. Once you can describe the moment when someone needs help, it gets easier to see which features belong in the first version.

Then talk to someone who has faced a similar problem and ask for their last example. If they describe the same moment in a different setting, you may have found a useful pattern. If the stories diverge, keep those differences in mind before designing for a broad group. An example with a clear context gives you something you can observe and test.
